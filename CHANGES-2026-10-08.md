# Life rebrand (2026-10-08)

- Everything you see is now **Life**: Life Launcher, Life Client, window titles, title screen,
  in-game menus, installer, desktop shortcut, Discord text, recordings ("Life <date>.mp4").
- New look: the "Life" theme (deep-water blue glass) over a liquid background in the logo's
  colours (ui/Liquid.java), and the "Life" accent: cyan buttons, switches and sliders with a
  liquid gradient and a wet shine (Theme.accentPaint / accentFill). Applied once automatically;
  the old wallpaper and custom gradient are turned off so the liquid shows.
- Icons: the LIFE art as a rounded app icon (window, taskbar, Linux menu, Windows .exe/installer),
  the Life tile in the sidebar, intro, lock screen and in-game title screen; the four-point Life
  star as the small mark (HUD watermark, menus, version list).
- Kept on purpose so nothing breaks: LifeLauncher.AppImage, life-launcher.jar, the data folder,
  saved logins, the "Life" cape design name. The Windows installer upgrades the old install and
  removes the old "Life Launcher" shortcuts.
- Discord shows the name of the Discord application: rename it in the Discord Developer Portal.
